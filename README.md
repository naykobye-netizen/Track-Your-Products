# Track-Your-Products

## What is Track Your Products?

Track Your Products is a web application designed to monitor and analyze product prices and stock availability across online stores. It combines price history tracking, product analysis, and an AI-powered assistant to help users make more informed purchasing decisions.

## What problem does this project aim to solve?

Buying technology products at the right time and from the right store can be challenging. Prices fluctuate frequently, stock availability changes, and finding reliable product information across different websites can be time-consuming.

Track Your Products aims to simplify this process by allowing users to track products through their store URLs, monitor price and stock changes, and analyze their price history.

Additionally, an AI-powered assistant will help users make better purchasing decisions by analyzing product information and customer reviews available online, providing useful insights and recommendations.
